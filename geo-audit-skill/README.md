# GEO Audit Skill

**Generative Engine Optimization Audits for AI Discovery**

## What is GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing your organization's visibility, authority, and citability within AI systems like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity.

Unlike traditional SEO (which optimizes for Google), GEO focuses on making sure:
- Your organization is discovered when users ask AI systems questions in your domain
- AI systems cite you as a credible source
- You appear in knowledge graphs (Wikipedia, Wikidata, Crunchbase)
- You're recommended by generative AI assistants

## What This Skill Does

This skill runs comprehensive **6-phase GEO audits** for any organization:

1. **Discoverability** — How easily can AI systems find information about you?
2. **Authority** — What are your EEAT signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)?
3. **Citability** — How likely are LLMs to cite you as a source?
4. **Semantic Understanding** — What do AI systems understand about your organization?
5. **Knowledge Graph Presence** — Are you in Wikipedia, Wikidata, Crunchbase, etc.?
6. **Strategic Roadmap** — How to improve over 6-12 months?

The skill evaluates and scores your organization on:
- GEO Score (0-100): Overall AI discoverability readiness

- Quick Win Potential: How many GEO points you can gain in 30/90/180 days
- ROI: Estimated investment required vs. expected impact
