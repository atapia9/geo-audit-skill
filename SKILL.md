# GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Audit Skill

## What This Does

Generates a strategic audit report evaluating how well an organization is positioned to be discovered, cited, and recommended by AI systems. Covers 6 audit phases: Diagnostics, Authority, Citability, Semantics, Knowledge Graphs, and Strategy.

## Input Requirements

Collect the following from the user:

**Essential:**
- Organization/Brand name
- Primary website URL
- Industry/Sector
- Geographic market (country or region)
- 2-3 known competitors (by name or website)

**Optional:**
- Depth level: `quick` (1 hour), `standard` (2-3 hours), or `deep` (4+ hours)
- Specific focus areas (e.g., "focus on knowledge graph gaps", "emphasize content quality")
- Output preferences (report-only, dashboard-only, or both)

## Process: The 6-Phase GEO Audit

### Phase 1: Discoverability Diagnostics (Score 0-100)
Evaluate:
- Website crawlability and indexation
- Site architecture and internal linking
- Content organization and semantic clustering
- Presence in search indexes

### Phase 2: Digital Authority (Score 0-100)
Assess EEAT signals:
- **Experience**: Relevant work history, credentials, case studies
- **Expertise**: Demonstrated knowledge, publications, specialization
- **Authoritativeness**: Domain authority, backlinks, institutional endorsements
- **Trustworthiness**: Transparency, testimonials, security signals

### Phase 3: AI Citability (Score 0-100)
Determine likelihood of being cited by LLMs:
- Content quality and verifiability
- Data presence (statistics, research, original insights)
- Schema.org implementation (Person, Organization, Article, etc.)
- Citation-friendly structure (FAQs, references, attributions)

### Phase 4: Semantic Understanding (Score 0-100)
Analyze what AI systems understand:
- Entity clarity (who/what is this organization)
- Relationship mapping (affiliations, projects, education)
- Role disambiguation (if multiple roles/titles exist)
- Potential semantic confusions

### Phase 5: Knowledge Graph Presence (Score 0-100)
Verify presence in:
- Wikipedia & Wikidata
- Google Knowledge Graph & Bing KG
- Crunchbase, LinkedIn, OpenAlex
- Industry-specific databases

### Phase 6: Strategic Recommendations (Roadmap)
Deliver:
- Top 10-20 critical findings
- Quick wins (high impact, low effort)
- 3-6 month roadmap with prioritized actions
- Competitive benchmarking

## Depth Levels

**Quick (1 hour) — Executive Brief**
- Web search only (focused, no exhaustive crawl)
- 4 core phases: Discoverability, Authority, Citability, Semantics
- GEO Score + top 5 findings with severity
- 10 high-impact quick wins (not 20+)
- Compact 5-page report + simplified dashboard
- Competitive benchmarking vs. 2-3 key competitors
- 30-day action roadmap (not full 6-month)

**Standard (2-3 hours) — Strategic Plan**
- All 6 phases with full depth
- Comprehensive web research across all channels
- Competitive benchmarking (3-4 detailed competitors)
- Full 6-month phased roadmap with effort/cost estimates
- 20-25 quick wins with hours and impact scoring
- Both full markdown report (10K-15K words) + interactive dashboard
- Industry-specific recommendations
- Risk assessment and competitive positioning matrix

**Deep (4+ hours) — Executive Transformation**
- All 6 phases with expanded depth and specialization
- Multiple rounds of targeted research (web + inference)
- 5+ competitor deep-dive analysis
- Detailed content audit with topic gaps and opportunities
- Schema.org and structured data audit (not just presence)
- Knowledge graph action plan (Wikidata, Wikipedia, KGs)
- Full deliverables: report, dashboard, competitor spreadsheet
- Supplementary files: content roadmap, schema checklist, publication strategy (if applicable)
- 12-month extended roadmap with investment projections
- Industry-specific modules (Academic: Publication strategy; SaaS: Developer positioning; Consulting: Thought leadership)

## Execution Strategy

### Step 1: Research Collection
Use web search to gather:
- Organization website content (structure, topics, updates)
- Backlink profile (mention quality, sources)
- Social proof (LinkedIn, Twitter, news mentions)
- Competitor positioning
- Knowledge graph entries (if they exist)
- Historical data (wayback machine if relevant)

### Step 2: Scoring & Analysis
For each of 6 phases:
1. Evaluate against specific criteria
2. Assign 0-100 score with justification
3. Identify 2-3 key findings (strengths & weaknesses)
4. Calculate overall GEO Score as weighted average

**Weighting (adjust based on industry):**
- Discoverability: 15%
- Authority: 20%
- Citability: 25%
- Semantics: 15%
- Knowledge Graphs: 15%

### Step 3: Competitive Positioning
Create comparison matrix with 3-4 competitors across all GEO factors.

### Step 4: Quick Wins Identification
Extract 15-25 actions that are <4 hours each with 2+ point impact.

### Step 5: Roadmap Construction
Organize into 3 phases (Foundations, Authority, Consolidation) with effort/impact estimates.

### Step 6: Report & Dashboard Generation
- Generate markdown report with all findings
- Create interactive HTML dashboard with visualizations and timeline

- GitHub presence, API documentation, developer citations

- LinkedIn articles and speaking engagements
- Industry awards and recognition
- Case studies with measurable results

**Academic/Research:**
- Google Scholar citations
- ResearchGate and Academia.edu profiles
- University affiliations and publications

**E-commerce/Retail:**
- Review platforms (Trustpilot, G2)
- Crunchbase company data
- Product ratings and testimonials

**Non-profit:**
- Wikipedia and Charity Navigator presence
- Grant database listings
- News coverage and media mentions

## Key Success Factors

1. **Specificity**: Provide concrete examples and action items, not generic advice
2. **Evidence-based**: Back every finding with research data
3. **ROI-focused**: Explain impact and effort for each recommendation
4. **Competitive context**: Show how organization compares to peers
5. **Actionable**: Report should be a roadmap, not just analysis
6. **Future-focused**: Include 6-24 month projections
7. **Time-conscious**: Respect depth level time commitments (quick stays ~1hr, standard ~2-3hr, deep ~4+hr)
